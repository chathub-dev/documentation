---
title: "Purchased Credits"
---

Purchased credits let you keep generating images and videos when your included allowance is insufficient.

- \$1 = 500 credits, starting at \$5 for 2,500 credits.
- Credits are valid for 3 months from purchase.
- Your included allowance is used first, followed by purchased credits.

<br />1. Open Credit Details On your account page, find Buy credits and click View details. 

![微信图片 20260908123158 114 3](/images/微信图片_20260908123158_114_3.png "微信图片 20260908123158 114 3")

2. View Your Purchases The Credit purchase details window shows each purchase’s status, total credits, used credits, and expiration date. To purchase credits, click Click here to buy more credits at the bottom. 

![微信图片 20260908142549 118 3](/images/微信图片_20260908142549_118_3.png "微信图片 20260908142549 118 3")

3. Choose an Amount Select a preset amount or enter your own. Review the total credits and validity period, then click Buy credits to continue to payment. 

![微信图片 20260908143357 120 3](/images/微信图片_20260908143357_120_3.png "微信图片 20260908143357 120 3")

After completing payment, return to **Credit purchase details** to view your new purchase. If it hasn’t appeared yet, click **Refresh**.

![微信图片 20260908143641 121 3](/images/微信图片_20260908143641_121_3.png "微信图片 20260908143641 121 3")
