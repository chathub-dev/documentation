**Text to video** is the fastest way to turn an idea into a short video from scratch — no input images required.

## How to Use Text to Video

<Frame caption="Text to video mode with model selection, format settings, and live credit estimate.">
  ![Text to video mode](/images/video-generation/text-to-video.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Text to video">
    In the [Video Generator](https://app.chathub.gg/video-generator), confirm that **Text to video** is selected in the mode bar at the top of the left panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your prompt">
    Describe the scene in **User prompt** (up to 5,000 characters). Focus on visible movement, camera motion, lighting, and sound atmosphere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose model and format">
    Select a model (**MiniMax H3**, **Seedance 2.5**, or **Seedance 2.0**), then set **Duration** (4–15s), **Resolution**, and **Aspect ratio**. Synchronized audio is included automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review credits and generate">
    Verify the real-time estimate on the **Generate** button (`Generate · Estimated X credits`), then click **Generate** or press `Ctrl + Enter` / `Cmd + Enter`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing an Aspect Ratio

Text to video supports six selectable aspect ratios:

| Aspect ratio | Recommended for |
| --- | --- |
| **`16:9`** _(default)_ | YouTube, desktop widescreen, presentations |
| **`9:16`** | Shorts, Reels, TikTok, vertical mobile video |
| **`1:1`** | Square social posts and profile visuals |
| **`4:3` / `3:4`** | Classic landscape or vertical portrait framing |
| **`21:9`** | Ultra-wide cinematic shots |

## Prompt Examples

You can copy and adapt either example to get started:

- **Product / café atmosphere:**
  > _A steaming ceramic pour-over coffee dripper on a sunlit oak counter, golden morning steam curling upward in slow motion, gentle dolly-in camera movement, warm café ambience with soft pouring water sounds._
- **Atmospheric nature scene:**
  > _A misty pine forest trail at twilight lined with mossy stones, warm golden fireflies slowly rising into the cool blue air, smooth forward glide camera movement, soft rustling leaves and gentle evening crickets._
