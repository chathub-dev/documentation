**Text to video** is the fastest way to turn an idea into a short clip from scratch — no source images required.

## How to Use Text to Video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Text to video">
    In the [Video Generator](https://app.chathub.gg/video-generator), make sure **Text to video** is selected in the mode switcher at the top of the left panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your prompt">
    Describe what happens in the scene inside **User prompt** (up to 5,000 characters). Focus on visible action, camera movement, lighting, and sound atmosphere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose model and format">
    Select a model (**MiniMax H3**, **Seedance 2.5**, or **Seedance 2.0**), then pick **Duration** (4–15s), **Resolution**, and **Aspect ratio**. Synchronized audio is enabled automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify credits and generate">
    Check the real-time estimate on the **Generate** button (`Generate · Estimated X credits`), then click **Generate** or press `Ctrl + Enter` / `Cmd + Enter`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing an Aspect Ratio

Text to video lets you pick any of six aspect ratios directly:

| Aspect ratio | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **`16:9`** _(default)_ | YouTube, desktop widescreen, presentations |
| **`9:16`** | Shorts, Reels, TikTok, mobile vertical video |
| **`1:1`** | Square social posts and profile animations |
| **`4:3` / `3:4`** | Classic landscape or vertical portrait framing |
| **`21:9`** | Ultra-wide cinematic shots |

## Starter Prompt Examples

Try pasting and customizing one of these prompts:

- **Cozy product / café scene:**
  > _A steaming ceramic pour-over coffee dripper on a sunlit oak counter, golden morning steam curling upward in slow motion, gentle dolly-in camera movement, warm café ambience with soft pouring water sounds._
- **Atmospheric nature shot:**
  > _A misty pine forest trail at twilight lined with mossy stones, warm golden fireflies slowly rising into the cool blue air, smooth forward glide camera movement, soft rustling leaves and gentle evening crickets._
