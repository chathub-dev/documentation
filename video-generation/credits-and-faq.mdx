## How Video Credits Work

- **Shared credit balance:** Video generation uses the same credit pool as [Image Generation](/cloud/image-generation). Your monthly allowance (**Pro: 3,000 credits/month**, **Unlimited: 10,000 credits/month**; Free accounts also receive a starter allowance) is always consumed first.
- **Top-up with Purchased Credits:** When your monthly allowance is insufficient, [Purchased Credits](/cloud/purchased-credits) (`\$5` for 2,500 credits, valid for 3 months from purchase) are used automatically.
- **Live estimate before you submit:** Cost varies by model, resolution, duration, aspect ratio, and reference image count. Once your settings and inputs are valid, check the exact estimate shown on the **Generate** button (`Generate · Estimated X credits`).
- **Pre-charge & settlement:** Credits are reserved when your task enters the queue. For Seedance models, final settlement adjusts based on actual model token usage when generation finishes.
- **Automatic refund on failure:** If a video fails to generate, **reserved credits are automatically refunded** (when completed within the same billing period).

## Troubleshooting & FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What image formats and file sizes are supported?">
    Upload **JPEG**, **PNG**, or **WebP** images up to **2 MB per file**. Files exceeding 2 MB or renamed with a mismatched file extension will be rejected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was my generation rejected for a real-person privacy issue?">
    Some video providers (including Seedance) block input images that appear to contain real people for privacy protection. Try using illustrated, stylized 3D, or non-human imagery instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my task fail with a sensitive content or copyright error?">
    Video providers run safety and copyright checks on prompts, uploaded images, generated visuals, and generated audio. Revise sensitive wording or remove copyrighted characters, brands, or recognizable music lyrics and try again. Reserved credits for failed tasks are automatically refunded within the same billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will closing the browser tab cancel my video generation?">
    No. Video generation runs asynchronously on ChatHub's servers. You can switch pages or close the tab at any time — your active progress and finished videos will be waiting when you return to [Video Generator](https://app.chathub.gg/video-generator).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many videos can I generate at the same time?">
    Each account can have up to **3 active tasks** (in queue or generating) running concurrently. Once a task finishes or fails, a slot immediately frees up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I turn off audio or generate multiple variations in one click?">
    Currently, each submission generates **1 video** with **synchronized audio enabled automatically**. To explore multiple variations in parallel, submit up to 3 tasks concurrently.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
