## How Video Credits Work

- **Shared credit balance:** Video generation uses the same credit pool as [Image Generation](/cloud/image-generation). Your monthly plan allowance (**Pro: 3,000 credits/month**, **Unlimited: 10,000 credits/month**; Free accounts also include a starter monthly allowance) is always used first.
- **Top up with Purchased Credits:** Whenever your monthly allowance is insufficient, [Purchased Credits](/cloud/purchased-credits) (`\$5` for 2,500 credits, valid for 3 months from purchase) are applied automatically.
- **Live estimate before submission:** Credit cost depends on the selected model, resolution, duration, aspect ratio, and number of reference images. Once your inputs are valid, review the exact estimate on the **Generate** button (`Generate · Estimated X credits`).
- **Reservation & final settlement:** Estimated credits are reserved when your task enters the queue. For Seedance models, the final amount settles based on actual generation usage upon completion.
- **Automatic refund on failure:** If a video fails to generate, **reserved credits are automatically refunded** within the same billing period.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What image formats and file sizes are supported?">
    Upload genuine **JPEG**, **PNG**, or **WebP** images up to **2 MB per file**. Files larger than 2 MB or renamed from unsupported formats will be rejected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was my generation rejected due to a real-person privacy policy?">
    Some video models (including Seedance) restrict input images that appear to depict real people to protect personal privacy. Try using illustrated, stylized 3D, or non-human imagery instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my task fail for sensitive content or copyright reasons?">
    Safety and copyright checks apply to prompts, uploaded images, generated visuals, and generated audio. Revise sensitive wording or remove copyrighted characters, brands, or recognizable music lyrics and try again. Reserved credits for failed tasks are automatically refunded within the same billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will closing the browser tab cancel my video generation?">
    No. Video generation continues in the cloud. You can switch pages or close your browser at any time — your active progress and completed videos will appear when you return to [Video Generator](https://app.chathub.gg/video-generator).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many videos can I generate at the same time?">
    Each account can run up to **3 active tasks** (in queue or generating) at the same time. As soon as any task finishes or fails, you can immediately start a new one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I disable audio or generate multiple variations in one click?">
    Currently, each submission generates **1 video** with **synchronized audio enabled automatically**. To explore several ideas at once, you can submit up to 3 tasks at the same time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
